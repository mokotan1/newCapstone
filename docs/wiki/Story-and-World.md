## Setting and characters

- Setting: 2025 Republic of Korea, inside the hidden mansion of a cult leader; outwardly charitable but concealing occult experiments. ([source_id: scenario:93cff884e57e](sources/scenario/the-unholy-of-mention-세계관--93cff884e57e.md))
- Protagonist: a former police detective driven by guilt over a lost partner; dry and cynical tone with investigative judgment under pressure. ([source_id: scenario:31ea9031cf8f](sources/scenario/the-unholy-of-mention-최종-시나리오--31ea9031cf8f.md))
- Cheshire: a mansion parrot who delivers riddles and testimony; not a simple animal but a witness to the mansion's truth. ([source_id: scenario:31ea9031cf8f](sources/scenario/the-unholy-of-mention-최종-시나리오--31ea9031cf8f.md))
- Antagonist Alfred: mansion owner and leader of the cult 'Those Who Advocate'; twisted faith after his wife's death leads to human sacrifice experiments. ([source_id: scenario:31ea9031cf8f](sources/scenario/the-unholy-of-mention-최종-시나리오--31ea9031cf8f.md))
- The cult poses as a university service club helping vulnerable people while recruiting victims for rituals and body-regeneration research. ([source_id: scenario:93cff884e57e](sources/scenario/the-unholy-of-mention-세계관--93cff884e57e.md))
