## Product premise

- The game is a 2025 Korea-set mystery thriller in a cult leader's mansion where the player gathers information, solves puzzles, and escapes. ([source_id: planning:35ada8161577](sources/planning/the-unholy-of-mention_컨셉-기획서--35ada8161577.md))
- The player goal is to explore the mansion, solve puzzles, uncover the mystery, and find an exit. ([source_id: planning:35ada8161577](sources/planning/the-unholy-of-mention_컨셉-기획서--35ada8161577.md))
- Core loop: room exploration, evidence and puzzle combination, and linear story progression guided by scenario beats. ([source_id: planning:35ada8161577](sources/planning/the-unholy-of-mention_컨셉-기획서--35ada8161577.md))
- Genre positioning: mystery thriller, occult horror, and exploration adventure. ([source_id: scenario:a73346ecb3d9](sources/scenario/the-unholy-of-mention-최종-시나리오--a73346ecb3d9.md))
